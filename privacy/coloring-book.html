Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** 16.07.2026 This Privacy Policy explains how Coloring Games for Kids ("the App") collects, uses, and protects information when you use the App. The App is developed by Dyakova Anna ("Developer", "we", "our", or "us"). By using the App, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

1. Information We Collect

The App does not require users to create an account. We do not intentionally collect personally identifiable information such as: * your name; * postal address; * email address; * phone number; * payment card information. Some limited technical information may be collected automatically by third-party services used within the App.

2. Children's Privacy

The App is designed for children and families. We are committed to protecting children's privacy and strive to comply with applicable privacy laws and Google Play Families policies. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Parents or legal guardians are encouraged to supervise children's use of the App.

3. Local Storage

The App stores user progress locally on the user's device. This may include: * completed coloring pages; * collected stickers; * unlocked content; * virtual currency; * application settings; * other gameplay progress. This information remains on the device and is not uploaded to our servers. If the App is removed or its data is deleted, locally stored information may be permanently lost.

4. Analytics

The App may use analytics services, including **Google Firebase Analytics**, to help us understand how the App is used. Analytics information may include anonymous technical data such as: * application version; * device type; * operating system version; * language settings; * country or region; * feature usage; * general performance information. This information is used solely to improve the App.

5. Crash Reporting

The App may use **Firebase Crashlytics** to automatically collect crash reports and diagnostic information. Crash reports help us identify bugs and improve application stability. Crash reports do not intentionally include personally identifiable information.

6. Advertising

The App may display advertisements provided by third-party advertising networks, including but not limited to: * Google AdMob; * Yandex Advertising Network. Advertising providers may collect limited technical information necessary to deliver advertisements, prevent fraud, measure performance, and improve advertising services. The collection and processing of such information are governed by the respective privacy policies of those providers.

7. Rewarded Advertisements

The App may allow users to voluntarily watch advertisements in exchange for optional in-app rewards. Viewing rewarded advertisements is optional. Advertising providers may process technical information required to deliver and verify rewarded advertisements.

8. In-App Purchases

The App may offer optional digital purchases, including: * premium access; * premium subscriptions; * sticker packs; * coloring packs; * virtual currency; * other digital content. Payments are processed exclusively by the applicable application store. The Developer does not receive or store payment card information.

9. Internet Connection

Certain features require an active Internet connection, including: * rewarded advertisements; * purchase verification; * premium content validation; * advertising services; * analytics; * crash reporting. Most coloring functionality remains available offline.

10. Third-Party Services

The App may use third-party services including, but not limited to: * Google Play Services; * Google AdMob; * Google Firebase Analytics; * Firebase Crashlytics; * Yandex Advertising Network. These services operate under their own privacy policies and terms.

11. Data Retention

Since the App does not maintain user accounts, the Developer does not store user progress on external servers. Locally stored application data remains on the user's device until it is deleted by the user or removed when the App is uninstalled. Anonymous analytics and diagnostic information collected by third-party services may be retained according to their own policies.
